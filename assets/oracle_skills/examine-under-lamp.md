## When to use
The task says to look at or examine an object *with* or *under* a desklamp.

## Procedure
1. Find the object and `take` it. You must be holding it.
2. Find the desklamp. It is usually on a desk, sidetable, or shelf.
3. `go to <the receptacle holding the desklamp>`.
4. `use desklamp 1`.

## Common failures
- Turning on the lamp before holding the object. The task only completes while you hold it.
- `examine desklamp` instead of `use desklamp 1`. The completing action is `use`.
- Moving the lamp. You do not need to take it.
